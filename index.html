      Loudness Auto-MasterLUFS target + true-peak limiter

      Export: measures integrated loudness exactly (BS.1770), sets gain, limits to −1 dBTP (4× oversampled peak detection, 5 ms lookahead) and re-checks. Live preview: adaptive gain from the running loudness.

      Ultra Limiter (L2-style)transparent true-peak brickwall

      4× oversampled true-peak detection, 3 ms lookahead with an S-shaped (double-smoothed) attack, dual-stage auto release so it recovers fast after hits but doesn't pump under sustained limiting. With Auto-Master on it is the limiter Auto-Master uses; on its own it only catches peaks (no loudness change).

      DitherTPDF · 16-bit WAV export

      Adds ±1 LSB triangular (TPDF) noise before rounding to 16-bit, so fades and reverb tails stay smooth instead of turning grainy. Repeatable (fixed seed). Last step before the file.
